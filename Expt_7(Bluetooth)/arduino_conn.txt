HC-06 MODULE       ARDUINO NANO
--------------------------------
VCC          --->  5V
GND          --->  GND
TXD          --->  D2 (RX)
RXD          --->  D3 (TX)
                 (Through voltage divider)

VOLTAGE DIVIDER:
Arduino D3 ---> 1kΩ resistor
                |
                +----> HC-06 RXD
                |
              2kΩ resistor
                |
               GND
